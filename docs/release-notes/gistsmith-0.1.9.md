Gistsmith 0.1.9 makes live translation read more naturally, keeps meeting pages tidier and speeds up the final pass.

## Improvements
- **Live translation by sentence.** Speech is translated a full sentence at a time instead of in short fragments, so repeated phrases and untranslated words no longer appear between pieces.
- **Fewer stray speakers.** People who spoke only briefly are grouped under "Brief speakers" on the meeting page. You can still open the group to name or merge them.
- **Other languages left out.** When you choose a meeting language yourself, speech that is clearly in another language is skipped in the transcript, the note, Ask and exports. You can still see the skipped parts on the meeting page. Automatic language mode works as before.
- **Faster processing after recording.** Audio is uploaded in a lossless compressed format, about half the size, so "Processing the full recording" finishes sooner with the same quality.
- **Ask.** Shift+Enter starts a new line, type "@" and press Tab to ask about a single meeting, and copy an answer with one click.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
