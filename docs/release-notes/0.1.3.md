A big update: a calmer new design, automatic updates, note and translation in your own language, an on-device mode, task tools (Linear, Jira, Notion) and many speed and reliability fixes.

**Installing over 0.1.2:** 0.1.2 can't update itself. Download this DMG once; from now on AI-Notepad updates automatically.

## What's new
- **Automatic updates.** AI-Notepad checks for updates and installs them (Settings › General). Crash reports stay on your Mac and are shown in Settings.
- **New design.** White, calm pages with soft gradient covers:
  - Home with a big "Start listening" card
  - meeting covers with a waveform
  - a centered search window (⌘K)
  - a focus screen while recording
  - redesigned Connections with real app logos
  - a setup roadmap
- **Your language.** Notes, live translation, "What did I miss?" and Ask answers are written in the language you choose (Settings › General). Each note remembers its language.
- **On-device mode.** No API keys needed: Apple Intelligence writes the note and Apple's speech recognition writes the live text, free and offline. It supports 15 languages. Finnish isn't supported, and the app says so instead of writing a poor note.
- **Tasks to your tools.** Create your tasks as Linear or Jira issues and send notes to Notion, Slack, Microsoft Teams or Discord. Nothing is sent twice, even from two Macs.
- **Meetings page:**
  - remembers the tab you were on
  - the meeting list can be hidden, like People and Ask
  - "Share via link" (iCloud)
- **Speed.** Much lighter while recording and playing back:
  - fixed a stuttering level meter
  - fixed whole-window redraws during playback that could freeze the time display
  - fixed a launch screen that could get stuck
- **Reliability:**
  - recording ends cleanly when the Mac sleeps
  - the end of your own speech is no longer lost when the connection drops at stop
  - marks you set on another device survive note rewrites and iCloud conflicts
  - more fixes from a full review

Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon.
