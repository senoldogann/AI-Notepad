Gistsmith 0.1.7 makes translated notes complete, keeps more of your meeting when something goes wrong, and fixes a long list of smaller issues.

## What's new
- **Translated notes look like the original.** Pick another language for a note and you now get the whole page in that language: tasks, summary, bookmarks, topic flow, decisions and glossary, with the same side cards. Exports and shared text in that language include the topic flow and glossary too.
- **Live text in your language from the first meeting.** New installs set the main meeting language from your Mac's language instead of Finnish.
- **Invites are clearer.** Settings › Account shows when your next invite reward is due, and the invite text explains both plans: a friend's first month is free on the monthly plan, or $5.99 off the first year on the yearly plan.
- **Free access accounts.** If your account has free access, Settings › Account says so, with no renewal date and no Manage button.

## Fixes
- If the final transcription stops part way (for example at the monthly limit), the rest of the meeting keeps its live text instead of disappearing from the transcript and note.
- Reprocess works for long meetings again; it no longer fails with "audio too long".
- When a renewal payment fails and the grace period ends, the app says so and points you to Manage subscription instead of saying your subscription ended.
- An invite code you typed but didn't apply is now checked before checkout instead of being dropped.
- Meetings recorded offline no longer keep your Mac awake.
- A failed reminder no longer hides your calendar meetings.
- A playback error only shows on the meeting it belongs to.
- Moving a meeting to the Trash from the list shows an error if it fails.
- Refreshing a note without a working AI setting keeps the old note instead of marking the meeting as failed.
- Sign-in error messages now tell you to continue with Apple or Google again.
- Many smaller fixes to text, accessibility and layout.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
