Gistsmith 0.1.11 makes the numbers in the app easier to read at a glance and lets your usage meters follow your accent colour.

## Improvements
- **Usage meters in your colour.** The monthly usage and trial meters in the sidebar and Settings › Account now use the accent colour you picked in Settings › Appearance. They turn amber when you're close to the limit and red when it's reached, and the text next to them follows.
- **Clearer account card.** Your monthly usage shows one main figure and the reset day; the longer explanations are behind an info icon. The usage notice shows your actual percentage, and the trial card shows when the trial ends.
- **Steadier numbers.** Counts, durations and prices use fixed-width digits, so they line up and don't jump as they change. Talk share minutes line up too, and anything under a minute shows as "<1 min".
- **Easier to read Diagnostics.** Dates are shown in your language instead of raw timestamps, and values that need attention, such as a denied permission or an unreachable server, are highlighted.
- **Tidier empty screens.** "Select a meeting" and an empty Completed list use a smaller placeholder, and a connected service looks the same everywhere in Settings.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
