Gistsmith now runs on macOS 15, adds a yearly plan and lets you invite friends.

## What's new
- **Runs on macOS 15 Sequoia.** Gistsmith now needs macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon. Recording, the live transcript, notes and Ask work the same as on macOS 26.
- **Yearly plan.** Subscribe for $49 a year (tax included), about $4.08 a month. The monthly plan stays at $5.99.
- **Invite a friend, get a month.** Subscribers find their invite code in Settings › Account. A friend who subscribes with it gets their first month free, and you get a month free once their first payment has cleared (about 30 days later).
- **A clearer end to the trial.** When your free trial is ending, Gistsmith shows what it did for you so far (meetings, tasks, hours) and both plans side by side.
- **Settings per account.** The Markdown folder, automatic export and connections such as Slack, Teams, Discord, Notion and webhooks now belong to the account you're signed in with, so two accounts on one Mac never share them.

## Improvements
- Connection cards line up at the same height, and a narrow note window keeps Refresh note and Recipe within reach.
- People: talk share is labelled, the person picker shows a menu arrow and the list is visible by default.
- Diagnostics are laid out as a tidy table, and the appearance sliders line up.
- Dark mode: meeting covers are toned down and the subscribe window matches the app's background.
- My tasks no longer repeats the meeting name under every task in a meeting group.
- After you open checkout, Gistsmith checks for your subscription less often and stops once you're back in the app.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
