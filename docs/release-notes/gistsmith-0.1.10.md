Gistsmith 0.1.10 adds an option to share anonymous usage statistics, which helps us find problems and improve the features you use.

## New
- **Share anonymous usage statistics.** In Settings › General you can now choose whether Gistsmith sends daily totals of which features you used (for example live translation, Ask, integrations and exports) and reliability counts such as connection drops or note errors. The setting is on by default and you can turn it off at any time. These totals are not linked to your account. Audio, transcripts, notes, meeting titles and names are never sent. The privacy policy has the details.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
