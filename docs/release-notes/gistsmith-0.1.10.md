Gistsmith 0.1.10 brings livelier covers, a cleaner Ask page and Diagnostics view, and an option to share anonymous usage statistics.

## Improvements
- **More vivid covers.** The gradients on the Home card, meeting cards and meeting pages are brighter and more colourful, especially in dark mode.
- **Cleaner Ask answers.** The AI model name no longer appears under answers.
- **Simpler Diagnostics.** Settings › Diagnostics now shows only what helps when reporting a problem: your app and macOS version, account, permissions, connection status and recent error areas.

## New
- **Share anonymous usage statistics.** In Settings › General you can choose whether Gistsmith sends daily totals of which features you used (for example live translation, Ask, integrations and exports) and reliability counts such as connection drops or note errors. The setting is on by default and you can turn it off at any time. These totals are not linked to your account. Audio, transcripts, notes, meeting titles and names are never sent. The privacy policy has the details.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
