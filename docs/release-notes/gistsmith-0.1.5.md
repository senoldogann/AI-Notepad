A faster live transcript, clearer limits and recordings that survive a lost connection.

## What's new
- **Faster live transcript.** Words appear in about a third of a second, with no large download on first launch. If the cloud service isn't available, Gistsmith switches to on-device transcription by itself.
- **Sign in inside the app.** Choose Continue with Apple or Continue with Google on the start screen; sign-in opens in a secure window in Gistsmith and the app is ready once you've signed in.
- **See your usage.** Meeting hours and your free trial are shown in the sidebar and in Settings › Account, with a calm notice before you reach a limit.
- **Updates in the app.** New versions download in the background; a short notice lets you restart when it suits you, never during a recording.
- **Diagnostics.** Settings › Diagnostics shows the app's state and lets you copy it into a support message.
- **Record without a connection.** If the internet drops, recording continues; the note is written once you're back online.
- **Import a recording.** Bring an audio or video file and get the same note as a live meeting.
- **Your meetings stay with your account.** Each account you sign in with sees only its own meetings, notes and people on this Mac; meetings you already have move to the first account you sign in with.

## Fixes
- "Start listening" no longer stays disabled without a reason while the speech model downloads; the download progress is shown.
- Ask explains that the free trial starts with your first meeting instead of showing an error.
- The interface opens in English by default; you can switch to Turkish or your system language in Settings.
- Clearer messages for every account and limit state.
- Fewer unexpected sign-outs: a session refresh interrupted by a lost connection no longer signs you out.
- English text no longer shows "1 meetings" and similar plural mistakes.

Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon.
